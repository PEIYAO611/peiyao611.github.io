Hi, I’m Peiyao

an HCI researcher and PhD student in Computational Media at UC Santa Cruz, where I’m advised by Dr. Norman Makoto Su. Before joining UCSC, I earned my master’s degree in Communication from North Carolina State University. And even earlier, I studied Animation as an undergrad. (Yes, I used to make things move frame by frame!)

My work sits at the intersection of workplace technology, social justice, and inclusive design. I use qualitative and mixed methods to explore how digital systems shape workplace communication, decision-making, and the voices of marginalized communities. My current research focuses on redesigning workplace concern reporting channels to be more emotionally responsive and socially accountable. I also collaborate with grassroots communities to co-design speech-AI tools that reflect the lived experiences and values of people who stutter.

Outside of research, I’m a huge sci-fi fan and a passionate LEGO enthusiast—building with bricks is my favorite way to unwind and spark creativity.

  • Designing Between Virtuality and Reality: Improving Inclusiveness in Hybrid Spaces (Accepted)

    Peiyao Liu, Chenxing Xie

    This experience report designs and introduces an application based on hybrid spaces and combines augmented reality (AR) to help improve the inclusiveness and accessibility of mobile application design. PlantNet is a hybrid application incorporating virtual plant cultivation and collection aimed at people with disabilities. This report discusses the possibilities of hybrid spaces to improve the inclusiveness and experience of applications.
