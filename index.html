Hi, I’m Peiyao

My research interest focuses on the evolving global work landscape and the integration of immigrant workers within tech industries. I am deeply invested in exploring the ethical dimensions and working conditions that shape the future of work, especially as they pertain to AI deployment in technologically underdeveloped regions. A significant aspect of my inquiry is committed to examining the role of social media as a powerful platform for marginalized groups to achieve social justice and amplify their voices, thereby contributing to a more inclusive and equitable digital and work environment.

  • Designing Between Virtuality and Reality: Improving Inclusiveness in Hybrid Spaces (Accepted)
    Peiyao Liu, Chenxing Xie
    This experience report designs and introduces an application based on hybrid spaces and combines augmented reality (AR) to help improve the inclusiveness and accessibility of mobile application design. PlantNet is a hybrid application incorporating virtual plant cultivation and collection aimed at people with disabilities. This report discusses the possibilities of hybrid spaces to improve the inclusiveness and experience of applications.
